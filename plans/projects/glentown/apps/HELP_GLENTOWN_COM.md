# help.glentown.com — GlenTown Help Center

**Status:** PLANNED.

## Purpose

Searchable support and product knowledge for residents, businesses, and professionals, with future Glen AI support integration.

## MVP

- role and audience-based help navigation;
- getting started, account, and sign-in guidance;
- community and content rules;
- buying and selling;
- services and bookings;
- orders, payments, and credits explanations;
- messaging, privacy, and safety;
- business setup;
- troubleshooting;
- contact and escalation path;
- legal and privacy links.

## Architecture

Prefer SEO and content-oriented technology with structured content, full-text search, analytics, and a maintainable editorial workflow. Documentation content should be reusable by Glen AI through a controlled retrieval layer later.

## Governance

Every article needs ownership, last-reviewed metadata, product-version relevance where needed, and a retirement or update process.
