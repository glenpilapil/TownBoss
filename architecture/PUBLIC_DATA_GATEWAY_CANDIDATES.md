# Public Data Gateway Approved Candidates

**Status:** APPROVED CANDIDATES / NOT IMPLEMENTED**

This registry supplements, and does not replace, `PUBLIC_DATA_GATEWAY.md` and the Adapter Roadmap.

## PHIVOLCS ArcGIS REST Services Directory

**Classification:** APPROVED HIGH-PRIORITY GOVERNMENT GIS INTEGRATION CANDIDATE / NOT IMPLEMENTED.

Potential GlenTown use is authoritative hazard/context discovery and public planning support. TownRealty/GeoPlotter may evaluate parcel/property intersection, proximity, and hazard/environmental context. Candidate layers include active faults, liquefaction, earthquake-induced landslides, volcanic hazards, and other suitable authoritative PHIVOLCS layers. NAMRIA, PAGASA, PSA, DPWH, MGB, and related authoritative government ArcGIS/public GIS services may also be evaluated.

Before ingestion: verify accessibility, authority/provenance, vintage, query capability, attribution/licensing/reuse, caching/freshness, and adapter/normalization requirements.

## DENR-BMB Protected Area Information System (PAIS)

**Classification:** APPROVED GOVERNMENT DATA / INTEGRATION CANDIDATE / NOT IMPLEMENTED.

Potential GlenTown/TownTraveler use is protected-area and ecotourism context; TownRealty/GeoPlotter may evaluate protected-area/environmental property context. Before ingestion: verify backing service/feed discovery, accessibility, provenance/authority, vintage, query characteristics, attribution/licensing/reuse, freshness/caching, and adapter/normalization requirements.
